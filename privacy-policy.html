PRIVACY POLICY.

How ARLEN FORGE may process information when you use our website, games and related services.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Important before publication: This is a studio-wide baseline policy. Before relying on it for a particular game, ARLEN FORGE must verify that the game's actual SDKs, permissions, advertising, analytics, platform services and data flows match these disclosures. Add any game-specific differences here and in the relevant app-store privacy/Data Safety declarations.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to websites and games published or operated by ARLEN FORGE (“we”, “us” or “our”). For privacy questions, contact arlenfacts@gmail.com.

2. Information we may process

The information processed depends on the particular game, platform and features you use. It may include:

  • Messages you send us: your email address and the information you include in a support or business enquiry.
  • Game and device information: technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, diagnostics and gameplay or app interaction events, where collected by the game or its service providers.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers, ad interactions and related device or usage signals where an ad service is used and permitted by the platform and applicable law.
  • Purchase information: transaction status, product and order-related information supplied by the platform or billing provider. Payment details are generally handled by the relevant platform or payment provider, not by the game studio directly.

Do not assume every category is collected by every game. The applicable game and platform determine which categories are processed.

3. How information may be used

Depending on the game or service, information may be used to operate and maintain the service, provide support, process and verify purchases, deliver advertising, understand performance, detect fraud or abuse, fix errors, protect users and comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising and third-party services

Some games may display advertising and may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. Those providers may process device, advertising, usage and interaction information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and provide related services, subject to their own terms and privacy disclosures.

Third-party services may also be used for app distribution, payment processing, hosting, diagnostics or other functions. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies. The specific providers used can differ between games and platforms.

5. Purchases

Some games may offer optional in-app purchases or other paid content. Purchases are handled through the relevant platform or payment provider. We may receive transaction-related information needed to validate a purchase or provide the purchased item. We do not ask you to send full payment card numbers by email.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information for money. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate our games or website, by platform and advertising partners where relevant, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, users and services. The actual recipients depend on the game and services it uses.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including support, security, legal and transaction-related needs. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Age suitability and child-directed features can differ between games. We do not use this general policy to declare that every ARLEN FORGE game is intended for children or that every game is intended only for adults. Please review the age rating and audience information for the specific game. Where a game is directed to children or is intended for a mixed audience that includes children, additional legal, platform and advertising requirements may apply and the game's disclosures and settings must reflect them.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can contact us at arlenfacts@gmail.com. You may also be able to manage advertising or privacy choices through your device or platform settings.

10. International processing

Our service providers or platform partners may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for such transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when the policy was most recently revised. Material game-specific differences should be disclosed in the relevant game's listing or supplementary notice where appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email arlenfacts@gmail.com and include the name of the game and platform concerned.
