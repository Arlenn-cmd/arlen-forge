PLAYER SUPPORT

WE'RE HERE TO HELP.

Find out what information to include when contacting ARLEN FORGE about a game, technical issue or purchase.

GAME HELP

Need help with a game?

For the fastest help, include the game title, platform and a clear description of the issue.

  • What happened?
  • What did you expect?
  • When did it start?
  • What device and operating system are you using?
  • What steps reproduce the issue?
CONTACT SUPPORT

Send us the details.

Use the studio support email. If possible, attach a screenshot or short screen recording. Do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information.

arlenfacts@gmail.com
PURCHASES

Purchase or billing questions

Mention the game, platform, approximate purchase date and order reference where available. Never include full payment card details.

BUG REPORTS

Help us investigate.

Describe the issue in simple steps and tell us whether it happens every time. Include your game version if visible.

Support availability and response times may vary. Contacting us does not guarantee that a particular feature, refund or fix will be provided.
